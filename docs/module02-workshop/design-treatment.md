## Game Concept

<!-- Explain why the working title fits. Mention alternative titles if useful. -->
[A big part of the game is going to be the grace periods between sections.]
[Some of the most important decisions you can make will be in these periods.]

<!-- Describe the setting, theme, and story or premise. Identify the characters
or the role the player takes. A game without a story can describe its situation
and player roles instead; do not invent a narrative just to fill this section. -->
[Player can take the role of one of four characters, all of which have a near identical reason to be
in this dungeon. The goal is to grab loot, get stronger, and make it out alive. The score will be tracked by
how much money the party/person is able to perserve by the end of it.]

## Gameplay

<!-- Explain the player's objectives, the conflicts or challenges, and how play
succeeds, fails, or ends. Describe the recurring decisions and actions, the rules
that connect them, and what changes in the game as a result. Game state means the
current situation, such as positions, resources, score, or available choices.
Describe the intended experience through those interactions. -->
[Each dungeon has small interactions and fights, complete a few of those and you reach the end with whatever loot
the player/s managed to grab. ]
[The player's goal is to grab whatever you can, and not lose any of it before exiting the dungeon.]

[Once the dungeon is entered (game start), the player/s fight through 4 interactions before the big boss at the end.
The player has to decide what to do with what loot they get, after the fights they must decide what to do with their stats.
Do they maintain their health, keep their equipment prestine, or possibly stash away a portion of their goods.]
### Setup And Start

<!-- Describe the starting state: how the play area and pieces are arranged,
what each player begins with, and how play starts. Refer to the pieces described
below rather than listing them twice. -->
[To start the game, the players just have to choose what role they'd like (swordsman, bard, mage, ranger). 
The person taking most aggro should stay infront of the party, characters with area of effect buffs should position
themselves accordingly. Players should keep in mind that different attack methods can make certain formations costly
to maintain (health wise mostly).]

### Core Gameplay Loop

<!-- Describe the recurring player choices and actions, the rules that connect

positions, resources, score, or available choices. -->
[At any given moment the player has to choose what gear to take with them, what to leave behind, and how much gold/scoare
they are willing to risk to progress. ]

### Ending And Outcome

<!-- State when play ends and how the outcome is decided. Include win/lose
conditions where applicable, or another clear beginning/end structure. -->
[A win is going to marked by keeping at least one party member alive and being able to afford the cheapest item
in the shop after the dungoen.]
