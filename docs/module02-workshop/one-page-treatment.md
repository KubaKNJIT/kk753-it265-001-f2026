# IT265 Module 2: One-Page Treatment

**Working game title:**

Write one coherent, one-page-length treatment. Use the four prompts to begin, then combine your answers into natural prose.

## Four-Sentence Core

1. **Premise:** Who is the player, what is the situation, and what problem matters?
2. **Experience and loop:** What should play feel like, and what does the player repeatedly decide or do?
3. **Goal and pressure:** What changes, what limits the player, and how does play end?
4. **Scope:** What makes this version distinctive, and what can you test first with affordable physical materials?

## Treatment Draft

The player will choose between 3-4 characters (mage, bard, swordsman, ranger). The idea is to progress through a dungeon by making certain decisions without getting themselves game ended. The idea is to have a small set of cards or some form of way to keep track of potential scenarios, roll a dice for scenario + player decision impacting the roll chance, and they either go into a fight or find something else to do (rare item find, npc interaction, etc.). A player can't just pick up everything they come by, class limitations, possibly a weight limit if I choose to go that route. I can test the behavior of choosing scenarios since I technically have a board I can repurpose as a "play-mat" of sorts and a bunch of dice. 

Add only useful audience, comparison, or loop-timing context. Keep the evolving Gameplay Timing Onion and reading reflection in your separate journal. Do not turn this into the fuller treatment or a feature catalog.

## Reader Check

If a reader is available, ask them to identify the repeated decision, resulting change, ending and first prototype. Revise unclear parts. Keep only the finished treatment prose in the published document; remove unused prompts and these preparation notes. Link its rendered page from your workshop index.
